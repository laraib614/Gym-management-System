System Features

Everything You Need to Run a Gym

A fully integrated management platform covering every aspect of gym operations — from member enrollment to revenue analytics.

👥
Member Management
Add, edit, and manage gym members with full profile details including age, gender, contact, join date, assigned plan, and trainer.
🏋️
Trainer Management
Manage trainer profiles, specializations, salary records, and track how many members each trainer is assigned to.
📋
Membership Plans
Create and manage Monthly, Quarterly, and Yearly plans with custom pricing. Track enrollment count per plan in real time.
🛒
Product Inventory
Manage gym supplements, accessories, and equipment with category filters, stock tracking, and low-stock alerts.
💳
Purchase Tracking
Record member product purchases, auto-calculate totals, deduct stock, and view complete purchase history with revenue stats.
📊
Gym Analytics
Live dashboard with total members, trainers, purchase revenue, membership revenue, grand total, and plan distribution charts.
🔐
Secure Admin Access
Single-admin login system with PHP session authentication, password hashing, protected routes, and secure logout.
⚡
Real-Time Data
All data loads live from MySQL via PHP REST APIs. Every add, edit, or delete reflects instantly without page refresh.
